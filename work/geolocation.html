IP Geolocation Infrastructure

2022 – present Fastly Primary owner

The system that tells Fastly's edge where in the world a request is coming from — and why getting it right matters more than most people realise.

Every time a request hits Fastly's edge, one of the first things the system does is work out where it's coming from. Not just country — city, region, ASN, connection type. That data feeds geo-fencing rules, content localisation, compliance controls, and routing decisions for millions of requests per second.

The challenge: IP address space changes constantly. Allocations shift, VPNs obscure origins, mobile carriers aggregate traffic across regions. Keeping the underlying data accurate is an ongoing engineering problem, not a one-time setup.

"You are (probably) not where the internet thinks you are" — the title of my internal conference talk on why geolocation accuracy is harder than it looks, and what we do about it.
